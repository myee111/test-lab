== What is a Quadlet?

A *Quadlet* is a small unit file that describes a container in the way systemd likes to see services. You write `nginx.container` with a `[Container]` section, and at boot (or on `systemctl daemon-reload`) a generator turns it into a regular systemd service — `nginx.service` — that pulls the image and runs the container for you.

Why that matters to you:

* Your container service starts at boot like any other service: `systemctl start nginx`, `systemctl status nginx`, `journalctl -u nginx`.
* The service is *defined as code* — one small file you can read, version, and hand to the next person.
* The `podman` system role is the supported way to create and manage those Quadlet files across your systems with Ansible.

[NOTE]
====
New to Ansible? You only need three words for this lab: a *playbook* is a YAML file of tasks, a *role* is a packaged, reusable bundle of tasks, and the *system role* is what Red Hat calls its supported roles. The `podman` role does the work; your playbook just tells it what you want.
